D&D Procedural World & Storyline Engine — Requirements Draft

1  Vision & Scope

Generate cohesive Dungeons & Dragons worlds and storylines on demand by exposing a set of tunable parameters (“knobs”) to the game‑master. The engine should output everything needed to run a campaign, from a high‑level plot arc down to encounter stat‑blocks.

2  Key Inputs (“Knobs”)

2.1  World Parameters

Geography / Terrain – biome distribution, climate bands, natural wonders

Culture Matrix – societal structure, predominant race(s), religions, politics, economy

Technology & Magic Level – low‑magic bronze age up through high‑magic steampunk

Historical Era & Timeline – founding myths, recent conflicts, major calendar events

Factions & Power Centers – kingdoms, guilds, cults, megacorps, planar forces

2.2  Narrative Tone & Themes

Primary genre focus (heroic fantasy, grimdark, mystery, political intrigue, etc.)

Moral alignment spectrum (black‑and‑white vs. shades of gray)

Difficulty / danger level (low, medium, deadly)

2.3  Character Parameters

Party Composition – size, level range, class distribution

Individual Seeds – race, background, personality traits, ideals, bonds, flaws

Inter‑Party Relationships – rivalries, romances, shared backstory hooks

4  Data Capture Requirements

4.1  User Input Interface

Web-based or standalone app with support for:

Sliders (e.g., magic level: low ←→ high)

Dropdowns (e.g., terrain type: forest, desert, mountain...)

Text boxes (e.g., custom faction names, specific character traits)

Multi-select lists (e.g., select allowed races or deities)

Preset themes (e.g., classic high fantasy, eldritch horror)

4.2  Input Categories (Simplified User Prompts)

World Settings

What is your world's name? (Free text input)

How many major regions or continents does it have? (Number input)

Choose the climates found in your world: (Multi-select: temperate, arid, tropical, polar, alpine)

What kinds of terrain are common? (Multi-select: mountains, forests, rivers, deserts, oceans, plains)

Any unique magical phenomena? (Multi-select: ley lines, magical storms, floating islands, none, other)

Cultures and Politics

How many major civilizations exist? (Number input)

What are the dominant races in each civilization? (Multi-select per civ: human, elf, dwarf, orc, dragonborn, etc.)

What type of government do they have? (Dropdown: monarchy, theocracy, democracy, oligarchy, tribal, other)

What is their economic base? (Dropdown: agrarian, mercantile, industrial, magical economy)

What do they value most? (Multi-select: honor, conquest, secrecy, freedom, knowledge, tradition)

Magic & Technology

How prevalent is magic? (Slider: None → Rare → Common → Ubiquitous)

What is the general tech level? (Dropdown: stone age, bronze age, medieval, renaissance, steampunk)

What is the dominant power source? (Multi-select: arcane, divine, psionic, elemental, technological)

Historical Background

Is this a young or ancient world? (Dropdown: ancient, mid-age, newly formed)

How many world-changing events are in the history? (Number input)

Describe any origin myths or creator gods. (Free text input)

Factions and Organizations

How many major factions exist? (Number input)

What types of factions? (Multi-select: political, religious, criminal, merchant guilds, academic)

Describe the relationships between these factions. (Free text or interactive relationship map)

Narrative Flavor

What kind of genre best describes your campaign? (Dropdown: high fantasy, grimdark, sword & sorcery, mystery, etc.)

How would you describe the moral tone? (Dropdown: black-and-white, shades of gray, ambiguous)

What themes are central to your campaign? (Multi-select: redemption, betrayal, survival, discovery, revolution)

How deadly should the world feel? (Slider: Low → Moderate → High → Lethal)

Party & Characters

How many players are in the party? (Number input)

What is the starting level range? (Range selector or dropdown: 1-3, 4-6, 7-10, etc.)

Which classes are allowed? (Multi-select: all core + optional subclasses)

For each character:

Name (Free text)

Race (Dropdown)

Class (Dropdown)

Background (Dropdown)

Personality traits, ideals, bonds, flaws (Free text or select from SRD list)

Are there any existing relationships or secrets within the party? (Free text or checkboxes: rivals, lovers, shared mentor, hidden betrayal)

Miscellaneous Options

What kind of naming conventions would you like? (Dropdown: real-world inspired, elvish-sounding, Norse-style, random fantasy, custom)

Measurement preference? (Radio buttons: Metric / Imperial)

Would you like to use a custom seed for consistency? (Optional text input)
